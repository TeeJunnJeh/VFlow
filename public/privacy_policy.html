Privacy Policy for VFlow

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VFlow collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our AI‑driven video generation platform. The UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) notes that individuals have the right to be informed about the collection and use of their personal data and that privacy information must be concise, transparent, intelligible and easily accessible. We aim to provide that transparency. This policy applies to personal data processed by VFlow in connection with our services.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and contact information: When you register for a VFlow account or contact us, we collect your name, email address, phone number, business name and login credentials. TikTok requires users to provide accurate and up‑to‑date information when creating accounts; we collect similar information to manage accounts.

  • User Inputs and content: We collect any text, scripts, images, video clips, audio files or other material you upload or input into the Service. We also collect the AI‑generated videos produced using our Service.

  • Usage data: We automatically collect information about how you interact with the Service, such as log files, device identifiers, IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, session durations and activity logs. We may also collect analytics on how videos perform on TikTok after upload.

  • Third‑party data: When you link your TikTok account, we may receive your TikTok user ID, account information and details about your posts. We may also receive feedback from TikTok regarding the status of uploaded videos and compliance with its policies.

  • Payment information: If you purchase a subscription or pay for premium features, our payment processors will collect billing and payment details. We do not store full payment card numbers; they are processed by a third‑party provider.

  • Communications: When you communicate with us (e.g., support requests, feedback), we collect the information you provide and may retain records of those communications.

  • Cookies and tracking technologies: We use cookies and similar technologies to enable core features and analyze usage. The EU ePrivacy Directive requires obtaining user consent before storing or accessing cookies unless strictly necessary for a requested service. We provide cookie notices and settings where required.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

How We Use Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  1. Provide and improve the Service: To authenticate users, generate AI videos, upload content to TikTok, process payments and provide customer support. Generative AI guidance recommends providing transparency about how models are trained and informing users what data might be collected. We may use your inputs and usage data to improve our AI models, but we will not publicly disclose your proprietary prompts or confidential business information.

  2. Communications: To send service‑related notifications, respond to inquiries and provide technical support. We may also send administrative messages about changes to our policies or features.

  3. Analytics and research: To understand how users interact with our Service, monitor performance, develop new features, and optimize video generation. We may create aggregated, de‑identified statistics that do not identify individuals.

  4. Compliance and safety: To comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, detect fraud, prevent prohibited or harmful content (e.g., content that TikTok prohibits) and protect the rights and safety of users and third parties.

  5. Marketing (with consent where required): We may send you information about new features or services. Where law requires consent, we will obtain it before sending marketing communications.

Legal Bases for Processing

VFlow processes personal data based on several legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract: We process account and content information to provide the Service you requested.

  • Legitimate interests: We process usage data and analytics to improve the Service, prevent abuse and protect our rights. Global data privacy laws emphasize that personal data must be processed fairly, transparently and only for legitimate purposes.

  • Consent: Where required (e.g., for cookies or marketing communications), we rely on your consent. The GDPR requires consent to be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous.

  • Legal obligation: We may process or retain data as needed to comply with legal requirements or respond to lawful requests.

Sharing and Disclosure

We may share personal information with:

  • AI service providers: We use third‑party AI providers (e.g., models like Sora) to generate videos. These providers may receive your prompts and inputs to generate outputs. Generative AI privacy guidelines warn that input data may be used as training data and that users may overshare personal information; therefore, we limit sharing to what is necessary for generating videos and contractually require providers to process data only to provide the requested service.

  • TikTok and other platforms: When you choose to upload an AI‑generated video to TikTok, we share the video and necessary metadata (e.g., captions, hashtags) with TikTok. TikTok’s Terms treat user content as non‑confidential and require compliance with its content policies; once uploaded, TikTok may process the video under its own terms.

  • Service providers: We engage vendors to host our infrastructure, process payments, provide analytics and support functions. These service providers may have access to personal data as needed to perform services on our behalf and are contractually obligated to protect it.

  • Legal and regulatory authorities: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or in connection with legal proceedings; or to enforce our Terms, investigate potential violations or protect the rights, property or safety of VFlow, our users or others.

  • Business transfers: If VFlow is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice of any change in ownership or uses of your personal information.

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. However, we may engage in practices that constitute “sharing” for targeted advertising; if applicable laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) define sale or sharing more broadly, we will provide additional disclosures and opt‑out mechanisms.

International Data Transfers

VFlow operates globally. Your personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we implement safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or rely on adequacy decisions to ensure an adequate level of protection.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy and to comply with legal obligations. Global data privacy principles require that personal data be kept no longer than needed. We determine retention periods based on factors such as the nature of the data, the purpose of collection, and legal obligations. When personal data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to be informed: You have the right to know how we collect and use your data.

  • Right of access, rectification and erasure: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct inaccuracies or delete your data. The GDPR grants individuals the right to access their data, correct inaccuracies, and request deletion.

  • Right to restrict or object to processing: You may ask us to restrict processing or object to certain uses of your data. Some privacy laws allow individuals to opt out of personal data processing for profiling or marketing.

  • Right to data portability: You may request to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format and have it transferred to another controller.

  • Right not to be subject to automated decisions: Under the GDPR, individuals have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect them. VFlow does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing; AI models are used to generate content but do not determine your legal rights.

  • Right to withdraw consent: If we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

  • California Privacy Rights: If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the CCPA, such as the right to know categories of personal information collected, the right to delete, the right to opt out of sale or sharing, and the right to non‑discrimination. We will provide specific disclosures in a supplemental notice for California residents.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We may verify your identity before processing your request and may decline certain requests if permitted by law.

Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, destruction or alteration. Generative AI privacy guidance recommends incorporating privacy‑enhancing technologies, data minimization and de‑identification. We adopt similar measures, including encryption, access controls, secure coding practices and regular audits. However, no system is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third‑Party Links and Services

Our Service may contain links to third‑party websites or services. TikTok’s Terms state that content on linked sites is provided for information only and that TikTok has no control over those resources; VFlow similarly is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third‑party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

We may modify this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The ICO advises organizations to regularly review and update privacy information. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or through the Service and update the effective date. Your continued use of the Service after the changes constitutes acceptance.
